Risk Analysis Findings

## Overall Portfolio

The dataset contains 32,581 loan records.

The overall observed default rate is 21.82%.

## Loan-to-Income Risk

Borrowers with loan-to-income ratios above 30%
recorded an observed default rate of 65.46%.

Borrowers below 10% recorded an observed default rate
of 11.54%.

## Previous Default History

Borrowers with a previous default recorded a 37.81%
observed default rate compared with 18.39% for borrowers
without a previous default.

## Risk Segmentation

The risk scoring framework produced four risk categories:

- Lower Risk
- Moderate Risk
- High Risk
- Very High Risk

## Portfolio Exposure

High and Very High Risk segments represented 39.61%
of portfolio exposure but 76.79% of defaulted exposure.

This indicates a significant concentration of observed
credit risk within these segments.
